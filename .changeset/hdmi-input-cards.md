HDMI inputs now appear as cards at the far left of the Home favorites strip instead of a separate
row. In Auto, a card appears only while its source is plugged in and sending a signal, so a
console that is unplugged or off takes no space. When an input reports an Xbox, Nintendo Switch or PlayStation (via its HDMI CEC name or SPD
InfoFrame), the card takes that console's name and colour, remembers it while the console is off,
and follows whichever console wakes up on a shared port.
